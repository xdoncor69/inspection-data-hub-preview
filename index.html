All Item numbers and source lines are kept. Catalog matches use catalog data. Unknown Items use available source product fields and are labeled Not in catalog. Only the administrator can choose to add them to the catalog. Quantities and weights are loaded later from the SAP delivery.

Order workflow

Create drafts using Import PO, Delivery First or From Picture at the top of Purchase Orders. Select a saved customer and review the product and EST values. Drafts remain pending administrator approval.

Approved for Inspection contains approved POs ready for delivery upload. Once a delivery is loaded, the PO moves to POs with Delivery. Open it to view deliveries, download originals or export the summary. Approval publishes the selected EST values; physical verification is a separate action.

The badge beside each stage title counts the orders currently shown, including any active search filter.
